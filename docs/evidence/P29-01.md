## Policy clarification

Repeated archive calls receive WORKSPACE_ARCHIVED through the ordinary access boundary. Restore of an already-live workspace and unchanged rename are no-ops when their version matches. Archive/restore retain every descendant row and its individual archive flag. Existing repeatable-read requests may finish against an earlier snapshot; new ordinary transactions reject the archived root.
