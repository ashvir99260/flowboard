Date: 23 October 2026. State: complete. Requirement: R119; preserves the R33 secret-handling boundary without completing its pending client work. Gates: G01–G07 and G11 as applicable; no client, UI or domain-configuration change.

Added the Node-only `@flowboard/logger` package and explicit API/common injection. HTTP, authentication, Redis, identity/tenant transactions, initialization, and API/worker lifecycle share structured stdout events.
